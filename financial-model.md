# HealthAI Assistant - Financial Model & Projections

## Revenue Model Overview

### Subscription Tiers & Pricing

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price | Features | Target Market |
|------|---------------|--------------|----------|---------------|
| Basic | R2,500 | R27,000 | 1 cancer type, 100 scans/month | Small clinics |
| Professional | R8,500 | R91,800 | 3 cancer types, 500 scans/month | Mid-size hospitals |
| Enterprise | R18,000 | R194,400 | All 5 types, unlimited scans | Large hospitals |

### Alternative Pricing
- **Pay-per-Scan**: R45 per scan (no monthly commitment)
- **Volume Discounts**: 10% for 1000+ scans/month, 20% for 5000+ scans/month
